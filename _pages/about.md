<h1 class="intro__title">Hello and welcome!</h1>
<p class="intro__lead">I am Guowei Huang (<span lang="zh-Hans">黄国伟</span>), currently a <strong>Postdoctoral Researcher</strong> in the Marketing Group at the University of Basel, Switzerland, and an <strong>Honorary Research Fellow</strong> in the Marketing and Management Science Division at Alliance Manchester Business School, University of Manchester. My research has appeared in leading business journals, including the <strong><em>Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science</em></strong> (FT50/ABS 4*), the <strong><em>Journal of Service Research</em></strong> (ABS 4), the <strong><em>Journal of Business Research</em></strong> (ABS 3), and the <strong><em>Journal of Small Business Management</em></strong> (ABS 3), as well as in the leading scientific journal <strong><em>Nature</em></strong> (Top scientific journal).</p> <p>My research focuses on two main streams. The first examines how firms, CEOs, and consumers communicate with stakeholders and how these communications influence stakeholder engagement and firm performance. In this stream, I primarily use machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and econometric methods to analyze large-scale unstructured data. The second stream focuses on developing and applying machine learning and large language model (LLM) methods to address methodological challenges in marketing research and practical business problems.</p> <p>I hold a PhD in Business and Management, an MSc with Distinction in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence, and a First-Class Honours BA in Business Management. My interdisciplinary background enables me to bridge marketing theory with advanced computational methods and AI.</p>
